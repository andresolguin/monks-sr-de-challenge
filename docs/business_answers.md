**# Business Analysis**



**This document summarizes the main business findings produced from the final analytical models for the May historical load and June replay dataset.**



**The analysis is based primarily on:**



**- `fct\_campaign\_performance`**

**- `fct\_acquisition\_performance`**



**## Metric Definitions**



**The main metrics used in this analysis are:**



**- \*\*Sessions:\*\* attributed GA4 sessions.**

**- \*\*Conversions:\*\* events marked as conversions in the source data.**

**- \*\*Purchases:\*\* purchase events, shown separately from the broader conversion metric.**

**- \*\*Revenue:\*\* value generated by purchase events.**

**- \*\*CPC:\*\* `spend / clicks`**

**- \*\*CPA:\*\* `spend / conversions`**

**- \*\*ROI:\*\* `(revenue - spend) / spend`**



**CPA therefore represents cost per source-defined conversion, not specifically cost per purchase.**



**ROI is an advertising return approximation based only on attributed purchase revenue and advertising spend. It should not be interpreted as company net profit because other costs and business margins are not available in the provided dataset.**



**Aggregate ratios are calculated from aggregate totals rather than by averaging row-level CPC, CPA, or ROI values.**



**---**



**## 1. Multichannel Performance**



**The paid media platforms show different strengths.**



**| Platform | Spend | Sessions | Conversions | Purchases | Revenue | CPC | CPA | ROI |**

**|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|**

**| Google Ads | 1,747,509.02 | 1,078 | 278 | 174 | 37,969 | 1.7487 | 6,286.0037 | -97.83% |**

**| Meta Ads | 421,316.93 | 1,261 | 314 | 113 | 27,543 | 0.5374 | 1,341.7737 | -93.46% |**



**### Interpretation**



**Google Ads generated:**



**- more purchase revenue**

**- more purchases**



**Meta Ads generated:**



**- more attributed sessions**

**- more conversions**

**- substantially lower CPC**

**- substantially lower CPA**

**- a less negative advertising ROI**



**Therefore, there is no single platform that dominates every business dimension.**



**Google Ads contributes more purchase volume and revenue, while Meta Ads is significantly more cost-efficient according to CPC and CPA.**



**Both platforms show strongly negative ROI under the challenge formula because advertising spend is much higher than the purchase revenue attributed in the provided dataset.**



**This result should be interpreted within the limits of the exercise dataset rather than as a complete profitability assessment.**

**## 2. Campaign Attribution and Effectiveness**



**Campaign effectiveness depends on the objective being evaluated.**



**### Highest attributed revenue**



**The campaign with the highest attributed purchase revenue is:**



**`META\_XYZ\_FEED\_RETARGETING\_US\_TEXAS\_SUMMER`**



**Results:**



**- Revenue: \*\*10,874\*\***

**- Conversions: \*\*40\*\***



**This campaign is particularly relevant when the objective is attributed purchase revenue.**



**### Highest number of conversions**



**The campaign with the highest number of conversions is:**



**`META\_XYZ\_REELS\_AWARENESS\_US\_FLORIDA\_SUMMER`**



**Results:**



**- Conversions: \*\*87\*\***

**- Revenue: \*\*2,078\*\***



**This demonstrates why conversion volume and purchase revenue should not be treated as equivalent measures of campaign effectiveness.**



**A campaign can generate many conversion events without necessarily generating the highest purchase revenue.**



**The final mart therefore exposes both:**



**- `conversions`**

**- `purchases`**

**- `revenue`**



**so analysts can evaluate campaigns according to the appropriate business objective.**

**## 3. Acquisition Channel Analysis**



**The available dataset supports reliable identification of:**



**- Google Ads attributed sessions**

**- Meta Ads attributed sessions**

**- sessions without an identifiable campaign**



**It does not contain enough information to safely classify sessions without a campaign as organic, direct, referral, or another acquisition source.**



**For that reason, these sessions are classified as:**



**`unattributed`**



**### Session-level results**



**| Acquisition group | Sessions | Conversions | Purchases | Revenue |**

**|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|**

**| Google Ads | 1,078 | 278 | 174 | 37,969 |**

**| Meta Ads | 1,261 | 314 | 113 | 27,543 |**

**| Unattributed | 324 | 80 | 43 | 10,015 |**



**The `unattributed` group is analytically important.**



**Discarding those sessions would remove:**



**- \*\*324 sessions\*\***

**- \*\*80 conversions\*\***

**- \*\*43 purchases\*\***

**- \*\*10,015 in revenue\*\***



**from acquisition analysis.**



**For this reason, the acquisition mart intentionally preserves those sessions instead of filtering them out.**



**No advertising spend is assigned to the unattributed group because the supplied data does not provide evidence linking those sessions to an advertising platform.**

**## 4. Attribution Approach**



**Campaign attribution is based on the GA4 `landing\_page` event selected for each session.**



**Two values are taken from the same event:**



**- `campaign\_id`**

**- `landing\_timestamp`**



**This prevents a campaign from one event being accidentally combined with the timestamp of another event.**



**The session is assigned to one six-hour advertising window using:**



**`batch\_window\_start <= landing\_timestamp < batch\_window\_end`**



**This means that exact boundaries are deterministic.**



**For example:**



**- 05:59:59 belongs to the 00:00-06:00 window.**

**- 06:00:00 belongs to the 06:00-12:00 window.**

**## 5. Hybrid Streaming and Batch Behavior**



**GA4 and advertising data do not arrive at the same time.**



**GA4 activity may become available before the corresponding Google Ads or Meta Ads six-hour batch.**



**The analytical model therefore preserves incomplete states.**



**A campaign window may be marked as:**



**- `pending\_ads`**

**- `ads\_available`**



**### `pending\_ads`**



**GA4 activity has arrived, but the corresponding advertising batch is not yet available.**



**The session activity remains visible instead of being discarded.**



**Advertising metrics such as:**



**- impressions**

**- clicks**

**- spend**

**- CPC**

**- CPA**

**- ROI**



**remain `NULL` until the Ads data is available.**



**### `ads\_available`**



**The corresponding advertising batch exists and the GA4 activity can be reconciled with it.**



**Repeated dbt execution progressively updates the analytical results as new source data arrives.**



**This behavior was validated during the June replay:**



**1. GA4 sessions appeared before their corresponding Ads batch.**

**2. Those campaign windows were exposed as `pending\_ads`.**

**3. The dbt build completed successfully while the data was incomplete.**

**4. After the corresponding Ads batch arrived, the same windows became `ads\_available`.**

**5. Advertising spend was not duplicated during reconciliation.**

**## 6. Data Model**



**The analytical model is organized into three layers.**



**### Staging**



**Incremental staging models:**



**- `stg\_google\_analytics\_events`**

**- `stg\_google\_ads`**

**- `stg\_meta\_ads`**



**Responsibilities:**



**- raw data cleanup**

**- deduplication**

**- incremental loading**

**- late-arriving data lookback**



**### Intermediate**



**Reusable transformation models:**



**- `int\_ads\_unified`**

**- `int\_ga4\_sessions`**

**- `int\_ga4\_session\_metrics`**

**- `int\_ga4\_campaign\_window\_metrics`**

**- `int\_session\_ads`**



**Responsibilities:**



**- standardizing Ads platforms**

**- building GA4 sessions**

**- calculating behavioral metrics**

**- campaign attribution**

**- six-hour window assignment**

**- Ads/GA4 reconciliation**



**### Marts**



**#### `fct\_campaign\_performance`**



**Grain:**



**\*\*one row per platform, campaign, and six-hour advertising window\*\***



**Used for:**



**- campaign performance**

**- spend analysis**

**- CPC**

**- CPA**

**- ROI**

**- conversions**

**- purchases**

**- revenue**

**- incomplete Ads state monitoring**



**#### `fct\_acquisition\_performance`**



**Grain:**



**\*\*one row per GA4 session\*\***



**Used for:**



**- acquisition analysis**

**- behavioral metrics**

**- preserving sessions without campaign attribution**

**## 7. Data Quality and Reliability**



**The dbt project includes tests covering:**



**- GA4 deduplication**

**- Google Ads window deduplication**

**- Meta Ads window deduplication**

**- at most one Ads match per session**

**- exact six-hour window boundaries**

**- campaign and landing timestamp consistency**

**- preservation of unattributed sessions**

**- pending Ads consistency**

**- pending Ads reconciliation**

**- campaign-window uniqueness**

**- non-negative metrics**

**- CPC, CPA, and ROI formulas**

**- zero and NULL denominator behavior**

**- reconciliation of impressions, clicks, and spend between the Ads layer and the final campaign mart**



**These tests are designed not only to validate static data, but also to protect the pipeline while the June dataset is still being replayed.**



**---**



**## 8. Main Business Takeaways**



**The principal findings are:**



**1. \*\*Google Ads generates more attributed purchase revenue and purchases.\*\***



**2. \*\*Meta Ads generates more sessions and conversions while operating with substantially lower CPC and CPA.\*\***



**3. \*\*Conversion volume alone is not enough to measure campaign value.\*\***

&#x20;  **The campaign with the most conversions is not the campaign with the most revenue.**



**4. \*\*Sessions without campaign attribution are material.\*\***

&#x20;  **Removing them would exclude 324 sessions and 10,015 of purchase revenue from acquisition analysis.**



**5. \*\*Partial data must remain visible.\*\***

&#x20;  **GA4 activity should not disappear simply because the corresponding six-hour advertising batch has not arrived yet.**



**6. \*\*Advertising spend must remain at advertising-window grain.\*\***

&#x20;  **Joining spend directly to session-level GA4 events could multiply spend and produce incorrect CPC, CPA, and ROI values.**



**7. \*\*The analytical layer should distinguish provisional data from available advertising data.\*\***

&#x20;  **The `pending\_ads` state makes that incompleteness explicit and allows later reconciliation.**
